Company Overview and Main Products

Intercom is a leading provider of AI-powered customer service solutions, offering a unified platform that combines messaging, help desk functionality, and a powerful AI agent named Fin. The platform enables businesses to manage customer conversations across multiple channels—such as live chat, email, SMS, and WhatsApp—through a shared inbox and ticketing system. Intercom’s Help Center allows companies to create and customize self-service support content without coding, with multilingual and multi-brand capabilities to serve global audiences.

Intercom’s pricing is structured around seat-based plans and usage-based AI resolutions. Customers choose from three main plans—Essential, Advanced, and Expert—each including access to the Fin AI Agent at a rate of $0.99 per resolution. The Essential plan targets individuals and small businesses, the Advanced plan adds automation and multilingual support, and the Expert plan offers enterprise-grade features such as SSO, HIPAA compliance, and service-level agreements. All plans include a free 14-day trial and unlimited live chat, in-app chat, support email, banners, and tooltips.

Refund Policy

Intercom offers a unique “Fin Million Dollar Guarantee” for new customers subscribing to the Fin AI Agent. If a new customer uses Fin in at least 250 paid conversations within the first 90 days and is not completely satisfied, they can request a refund of up to $1 million USD, no questions asked. The refund applies only to Fin-related spend and is available only once per customer. Certain promotional plans, such as Early Stage or Startup Partner Program, are not eligible for this guarantee.

Customer Support & Contact Information

Intercom provides customer support through multiple channels embedded within its platform. Users can access support via live chat (Messenger), email, and in-app messaging, all of which are available at no additional cost. The Help Center is fully integrated, allowing users to search for articles directly within conversations and receive AI-powered suggestions. For account management tasks—such as canceling subscriptions, changing plans, or managing seats—customers can navigate to their workspace settings under Subscription and Billing. For more complex or contract-related issues, users are encouraged to contact their Account Manager via the Messenger.

How to Buy/Engage

To start using Intercom, customers can sign up for a free 14-day trial of any plan. After the trial, they select a plan—Essential, Advanced, or Expert—and pay per seat, billed either monthly or annually. Fin AI Agent usage is billed separately at $0.99 per resolution. Customers can also integrate Fin with existing helpdesk systems like Zendesk or Salesforce, paying only for Fin resolutions without additional platform fees. Seat counts, add-ons, and plan changes can be managed directly in the workspace settings, with upgrades taking effect immediately and downgrades applying at the next billing cycle or renewal.
