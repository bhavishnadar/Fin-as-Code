**Intercom** is a customer communications platform built to help businesses build better relationships with their customers. Founded in 2011, Intercom started with a simple idea: most customer communication tools were reactive, fragmented, and impersonal. Intercom set out to change that by creating a single, integrated platform where businesses could communicate with customers in a more human, proactive, and contextual way.

At its core, Intercom brings together messaging, support, and automation. Instead of relying solely on traditional ticketing systems, Intercom enables real-time conversations through chat, email, and in-app messaging. This allows companies to meet customers where they are, whether they are browsing a website, using a product, or looking for help.
